About

I work at the intersection of two things most businesses need but rarely find in one person: someone who can build and maintain the technology, and someone who understands how to find and reach customers.

On the development side, I build and fix backend functionality, integrate APIs, work with databases, and handle the practical, unglamorous work of keeping a web application running well. On the sales and lead-generation side, I've spent time doing outbound prospecting, researching businesses and decision-makers, and helping companies build and organize the lists they need to grow.

I'm not positioning myself as a large agency or a twenty-year veteran. I'm straightforward about what I can do, and I focus on delivering real, usable results rather than overselling.
